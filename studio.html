About the studio

Fried Egg Studios makes games and software that are simple to start and hard to put down.

Who we are

Fried Egg Studios is an independent studio registered in England and Wales. We work in two ways: we publish our own mobile games, and we build games, apps and software for clients.

Our first titles are Touch Nut and Boo: Back Home, both coming to iPhone. Alongside games, the studio also builds social apps and other software.
